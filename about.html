The Story Behind the Camera

I'm Atul — a social media marketer, content creator, photographer and video editor based in Barwani, Madhya Pradesh. Over the last three years I've helped restaurants, schools, hospitals, real estate companies and local businesses turn ordinary online presence into brands people actually want to follow.

My work sits at the intersection of strategy and storytelling — I don't just point a camera and shoot; I plan content that fits a brand's voice, then edit and post it to actually move the numbers that matter.

My Approach
